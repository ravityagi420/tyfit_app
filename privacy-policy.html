Privacy Policy

A clear summary of how Tyfit collects and uses your information.

Who we are

Tyfit is a fitness and nutrition platform designed to help users plan meals, training, and progress tracking. This page is a template and should be reviewed with legal counsel before publishing.

What data we collect

We may collect account details, profile fields, nutrition entries, training plans, body metrics, and progress records you add in the app.

Why we collect it

We collect data to provide your personalized experience, improve app features, and offer account support.

Supabase as backend provider

Tyfit uses Supabase for authentication, database, and storage. Data is processed according to your Supabase project configuration and applicable laws.

Google login

If you sign in with Google, Tyfit receives basic identity information (such as name and email) necessary for login and account linking.

Diet, training, profile and progress data

Your meal plans, food logs, training schedules, profile preferences, and progress entries are stored to power key Tyfit features.

Data storage

Data is stored in cloud infrastructure configured for Tyfit. Exact regions and retention windows should be documented in your production legal policy.

User rights

You may request export, correction, or deletion of personal data using the Privacy & Data page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: privacy@tyfit.app (placeholder).
