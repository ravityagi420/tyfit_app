Data Processing Info

A simple explanation of what data Tyfit stores and why.

What user data Tyfit stores

Tyfit may store account identity data, profile details, diet plans, training plans, body metrics, and progress history entered by users.

Why it is stored

Data is stored to provide core app features, personalize your experience, and help maintain account continuity across sessions.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in cloud services configured for Tyfit, including Supabase database and storage systems.

How users can request export, deletion, correction

Users can open the Privacy & Data page and submit requests for data export, correction, or deletion. Requests are logged and processed through Tyfit support and compliance workflows.
