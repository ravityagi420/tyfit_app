Cookie Policy

How Tyfit uses cookies and similar storage technologies.

Essential cookies and session storage

Tyfit uses essential browser storage and session mechanisms to keep you signed in, protect your session, and support core app functionality.

Google login related cookies

When using Google login, authentication providers may set required cookies for secure sign-in and session validation.

No advertising cookies (unless added later)

Tyfit currently does not use advertising cookies. If advertising technologies are introduced in the future, this policy should be updated clearly.

User choices

You can control cookies through browser settings, but disabling essential cookies may impact login and app usability.
